# Child Safety Standards – Giza Social

**App:** Giza Social
**Developer:** CYPARTA SOFTWARE HOUSE FOR DIGITAL SOLUTIONS
**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

## Our commitment
Giza Social, developed by CYPARTA SOFTWARE HOUSE FOR DIGITAL SOLUTIONS, has **zero tolerance** for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). These standards apply to all users and all content on Giza Social.

## What is strictly prohibited
- Any content that sexually exploits or abuses minors, including Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form (images, videos, text, links, or AI-generated content).
- Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to contact a minor for sexual purposes.
- Sextortion, trafficking, or any other form of child exploitation.
- Sharing, requesting, promoting, or linking to any of the above.

## How we enforce these standards
- Content moderation and in-app reporting tools.
- Immediate removal of any violating content.
- Permanent banning of every account involved in CSAE.
- Reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national authorities, and cooperating fully with law enforcement.

## How to report
Users can report content and accounts directly inside the Giza Social app using the **Report** option, without leaving the app. You can also contact our child safety team directly:

**Child Safety Contact:** Giza Social Trust & Safety Team – support@cyparta.com

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact your local police or emergency services right away.

## Compliance
Giza Social complies with all applicable child safety laws and with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.
